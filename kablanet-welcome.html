Step one

Signing in the first time

Your account already exists — you don't need to sign up or create a company. It takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Go to kablanet.com

    It works in any browser, on a laptop or on your phone. There's nothing to install.

  2. 2

    Sign in with your email address

    Use the same email this invitation was sent to, and the temporary password from that email. If it doesn't work, click Forgot password and we'll email you a reset link.

  3. 3

    Change your password right away

    Open Settings in the left menu, stay on the User Settings tab, and use Change Password on the right. While you're there, check that your name and contact details are right — they appear on the documents you send.

Please don't skip step 3. The temporary password is only meant to get you in the door. Your account holds real money figures, so give it a password only you know.

How it's set up

It's your business in here

The most useful thing to understand on day one: this isn't a portal for one customer's jobs. It's your own set of books, and it works the same for every customer, every crew and every supplier you deal with.

Money in

Your customers

Builders, developers, homeowners — whoever you invoice. You bill them, and Kablanet tracks what's still outstanding.

you bill

Your company

You

Your projects and your crews in the middle. You're the admin here — everything in the account is yours to set up your way.

you pay

Money out

Subs & suppliers

The contractors you hand work to and the merchants you buy from. What you owe them sits against the same job.

Both sides of that picture live on the same project, which is the whole point: once a job has your billing on one side and your labour, subs and materials on the other, Kablanet can tell you what the job actually left you — not just what it was worth.

To give you a running start, we copied across some of your recent work with Lux Builders — the projects, the amounts invoiced and the payments received. Treat it as an example to learn on, not the limit. Add your own customers and jobs whenever you're ready, and run the rest of your business through here the same way.

You'll also find a Demo Customer and a Demo Project in the list. That's a practice job we left you on purpose — raise a test invoice against it, break it, delete it. It's not real work and it doesn't affect your real totals.

Spot something in the pre-loaded jobs that doesn't match your records — a wrong amount, a missing job, a payment you never received? Don't work around it. Tell us and we'll fix it at the source.

Straight answers

The questions everyone asks

Is this only for the jobs you pre-loaded?

No — those are a head start, nothing more. Kablanet is built to run your whole business: every customer you bill, every subcontractor you hire, every supplier you buy from. The more of it you put in, the more the numbers on your dashboard are actually your numbers.

Can my customers see inside my account?

No. Your records are yours — another company can't sign in to your account or read your figures, and what you add is invisible to them. A customer only ever sees a document you deliberately send them. The only people who can get in for support are the Kablanet team.

Can I use it for my own subs and suppliers too?

That's half of what it's for. Add your subcontractors and give them jobs on a project; log your material invoices against the same project. Accounts Payable then shows what you owe and when — the mirror image of what you're chasing.

A number doesn't match my records. What now?

If it's in the work we pre-loaded, tell us rather than editing around it — we copied that from the shared job records, so if something is off it's worth tracing back to the source. Anything you enter yourself, you can simply fix.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It's a website, not an app you install — open kablanet.com in your phone's browser and sign in. Add it to your home screen if you want it a tap away. The screens are built for a phone as well as a laptop.

Can my office manager or bookkeeper have their own login?

Yes, and they should — you're the admin of your company, so you can invite your people in Settings and choose how much each of them sees. Don't share one password around.

Do I have to sign anything, or chase signatures?

Not unless you want to. Your account is set up in invoice mode, so there are no contracts or change orders to sign. An invoice can be sent out for signature if you tick Require Signature when you create it — your customer signs it in their browser and you counter-sign — but nothing requires it, and it's off by default.
